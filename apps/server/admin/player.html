Tag Scores

How tag scores work

Tag scores use an Elo-style rating per topic. They drive two things: question selection (the main catch-up mechanic) and a small score multiplier.

ScoreMeaningDifficultyMultiplier
1900 (max)Very strong1 (easy for them)0.95x
1500 (baseline)Unknown / average3 (neutral)1.01x
1100 (min)Very weak5 (hard for them)1.10x

Question selection is the main lever: trailing players get questions they're strong at, leaders get questions they're weak at. The score multiplier range (0.95x-1.10x) is intentionally small.

Decay: scores drift back toward 1500 over time if a player doesn't play, with a half-life of ~10 games.
